Pocket Guitar Tuner

A little less friction. A little more music.

Support

Tap Start tuning and allow microphone access. Pluck one open string at a time, muting the others. Tune up when flat and down when sharp. Green means within 5 cents of the target.

Choose Standard (E A D G B E) or Drop D (D A D G B E), from thickest to thinnest. Auto detects the closest guitar string; tap a string to lock it. The reference pitch defaults to A4 = 440 Hz.

If the microphone is unavailable, enable access in iPhone Settings and close other apps using audio. Pause and restart tuning after an interruption. For help, open a support issue. Do not include private information in public issues.

Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Pocket Guitar Tuner is provided by Jake Johnson. The app processes microphone audio on your device to identify musical pitch. It does not save recordings, upload audio, collect personal information, use advertising, use analytics, or require an account.

Microphone access is used only while tuning is active. Capture stops when you pause tuning or put the app in the background. You can revoke microphone permission in your device settings at any time.

There is no app data stored on our servers to delete. Apple may separately process App Store and diagnostic information under its own policies. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process ordinary web request information under its privacy policy. Information you choose to post in public GitHub support issues is publicly visible.

Questions about privacy can be raised through the support page.
